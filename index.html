Takeaways

The bikeshare data indicates a flow of riders towards the city center during AM peak hours and a flow outwards during PM peak hours. Arrivals and departures remain balanced at most stations during working hours even though ridership remains fairly high; showing balanced directional flows of bike-traffic. Together, this suggests that the bikeshare system is well-utilized by outer-city commuters as well as inner-city users relying on it for intracity travel. The transfer of bikes from outer-city areas to the inner-city stations in the morning could pose challenges for maintaining consistent access for outer-city users during working hours. However, these outer-city stations also see high arrivals after work hours, suggesting that there may not be a huge need to redistribute bikes at night to serve commuters the next day.
